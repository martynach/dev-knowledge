# Azure cost review — 2026-11-18

**Cloud setup is blocked under a strict guaranteed-zero-cost requirement.** No resources were provisioned. Free grants are conditional and several proposed dependencies can incur charges without a hard spending cap. Do not set `enable_cloud`, `cost_risk_accepted`, `CloudDeploymentEnabled` or `CostSafetyApproved` until the account owner explicitly approves the reviewed exposure. A budget alert is not a hard cap.

The following official sources were reviewed on 2026-11-18. Recheck current region, account offer, eligibility and all shared subscription consumption immediately before any setup. No claim here guarantees an entitlement for your account.

## Proposed resources and dependencies

- **Resource group and managed identities**: two user-assigned identities and one resource group. No separate usage charge for the objects themselves; their workloads still bill. Each identity gets only its environment database's built-in Cosmos Data Contributor role. See [managed identities](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/managed-identities-azure-resources/overview).
- **Static Web Apps Free**: two sites, SKU Free, standard generated hostnames. Current documented Free limits include 100 GB bandwidth per subscription, 500 MB total storage and 250 MB per environment. Free bandwidth exhaustion stops serving the site; do not upgrade automatically. DEV + PROD share the subscription bandwidth allowance. No custom domain purchase or DNS hosting is included. Verify [quotas](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/static-web-apps/quotas) and [pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/app-service/static/).
- **Container Apps Consumption**: two apps, one shared managed environment, 0.25 vCPU / 0.5 GiB per replica, min 0 / max 1 each. Current monthly subscription grants are 180,000 vCPU-seconds, 360,000 GiB-seconds and 2 million requests. Both apps and any other apps share these grants. Scale-to-zero and max replicas reduce exposure but do NOT cap aggregate CPU time, requests, egress or spending. Public traffic can exhaust allowances; even one active replica can exceed the free grant. No enforceable zero-spend cap is supplied. [Pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/container-apps/), [billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/container-apps/billing).
- **Cosmos DB provisioned NoSQL Free Tier**: one eligible account, opted into Free Tier at creation, one region, session consistency, no autoscale or automatic failover. Two databases at 400 RU/s each consume 800 RU/s of the account's documented 1,000 RU/s allowance. DEV/PROD share the 25 GB free storage allowance. Only one Free Tier account per subscription is eligible. The throughput cap of 1,000 limits provisioning, not storage charges, backup charges or all billing. Storage can exceed 25 GB; historical throughput/storage can affect future minimums. No hard zero-spend cap. Review [Free Tier](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cosmos-db/free-tier), [limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cosmos-db/concepts-limits) and [pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/cosmos-db/standard-provisioned/). Independent DEV/PROD accounts in one subscription cannot both claim the single Free Tier entitlement. The generated alternative uses separate databases and database-scoped identities in one account; approve this weaker infrastructure isolation explicitly before setup.
- **Entra External ID**: external customer tenant, SPA and API registrations, base external identity usage only. The core offer documents the first 50,000 monthly active users free; premium features, SMS, MFA choices, linked subscription rules and extra usage require separate assessment. No claim of account eligibility or hard cap. Avoid paid identity add-ons. Confirm [pricing and billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/external-id/external-identities-pricing) and tenant availability before signup. The user's subscription/payment prerequisites must be checked manually.
- **Image registry**: Azure Container Registry Basic is billable and is intentionally absent from Terraform. [ACR pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/container-registry/). Proposed alternative: an existing approved public OCI registry such as GHCR, with no secrets in layers. Public pull hosting and CI push terms/quotas must be independently verified; private registry hosting is not silently substituted. No registry account or repository was created. [GHCR billing](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/managing-billing-for-your-products/managing-billing-for-github-packages/about-billing-for-github-packages).
- **Monitoring**: Log Analytics, Application Insights and paid diagnostic export are NOT provisioned; the Container Apps environment has no log destination. Enabling them can charge for ingestion, retention or queries. Azure platform metrics still require account-specific verification. [Azure Monitor pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/monitor/). Structured API console logs are implemented; durable cloud audit retention is an explicit limitation.
- **Terraform state**: local encrypted workstation storage is the default strategy. Blob storage accounts, transactions, retained versions, locks and networking can be billable and are NOT created. A shared remote backend requires separate explicit cost/security approval. Do not put state in Git or pipeline artifacts. [Storage pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/storage/blobs/).
- **Azure DevOps**: Azure Repos policies, pipeline artifacts, artifact retention and job entitlements depend on organization offer. Microsoft-hosted free parallelism may require a request and may not be granted; without it CI is blocked. Do not buy hosted capacity or start paid agents. An existing approved self-hosted agent is an alternative only after reviewing its own costs and security. [Parallel jobs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/devops/pipelines/licensing/concurrent-jobs), [Azure DevOps pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/devops/azure-devops-services/).
- **Networking and ancillary services**: public HTTPS generated endpoints only. No NAT gateway, VNet, private endpoint, Front Door, API Management, Key Vault, paid DNS zone, storage service or external search is included. Egress, public environment/platform networking charges, subscription defaults, Defender plans, backups and regional terms may introduce charges. Review [bandwidth pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/bandwidth/) and subscription-wide enabled services. TLS at the managed endpoints does not remove those risks.
- **Subscription and payment**: Azure eligibility, credit card requirements, trial expiration and spending-limit behavior depend on the user's offer. A monthly budget notification does not stop resources. Do not change offer, remove a spending limit or accept a payment agreement on the user's behalf. [Spending limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/spending-limit).

## Decision

The complete local product requires no Azure account. Continue using local .NET/Angular or Docker for a reliably cloud-free option. The generated Azure target is a reproducible proposal, not an approved free deployment. If absolute zero-charge exposure remains mandatory, keep all cloud flags off and do not provision. Alternatives requiring a decision are an isolated free sandbox with enforced subscription spending limits (if actually available and verified for these services), or explicit consent to bounded but nonzero risk. Do not treat scale limits, budget alerts or Free Tier labels as that consent.
